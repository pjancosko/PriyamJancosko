About

Practice and Background

I work across augmented reality, animation, video, and mixed media to create experiences that ask viewers to look again at what surrounds them. My practice centers on memory, belonging, and the experience of cultural migration. How meaning accumulates in space. How a place holds what its people have passed through it.

My digital works use layered visual fields, abstract and atmospheric, rooted in color and form, as surfaces that carry language, movement, and time. Built using A-Frame and WebAR, the work is designed to be encountered in public: intimate without being private, personal without requiring explanation.

I teach Art and Technology at a New Jersey public school, where I also run a cultural club that brings students together around shared creative practice. Teaching has sharpened my attention to how people encounter work they did not choose to stand in front of, which is the central condition of all public art.
