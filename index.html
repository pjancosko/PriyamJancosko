About

New Jersey-based artist and educator whose work explores migration, memory, and environment as shared conditions of people, places, and things. Drawing on her experience as an immigrant, she examines how movement across time and space transforms everyday life through fragments of memory, the significance of everyday objects, and the slow evolution of inhabited spaces. Her foundation is in painting, expanded through digital illustration, large-scale public work, animation, 3D modeling, and Web AR built on open-source platforms accessible without an app.

Jancosko teaches Applied Technology at Monmouth Regional High School, where she founded Kulture Lab, a student-led club celebrating the diverse cultures of the school community. Her work has been published in Brown Anthology and Battalion (SideKick Books, England) and selected for exhibition at the Monmouth Museum.
