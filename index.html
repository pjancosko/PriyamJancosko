About

I work across augmented reality, 2D and 3D animation, mixed media, and digital art. My practice is rooted in everyday mundane things that become special through memory of those we are surrounded by and the culture of objects.

Built using A-Frame and open source libraries. Inclusive platforms matter to me as an educator. The work is designed to be encountered anywhere, by anyone.

I teach Art and Technology at a New Jersey public school, where I also run Kulture Lab, a student cultural club. Teaching has shaped my perspective to elevate cultural stories for public art.
